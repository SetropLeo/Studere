# AI Regulation and Risk Frameworks

> **Summary:** The NIST AI RMF is a voluntary US framework; the EU AI Act is binding law with four risk levels and phased dates, shifted by the 2026 AI Omnibus. Fast-changing: all of this comes from official summary pages, not the legal text.

## What it is

NIST AI RMF (NIST pages):
- Voluntary framework to manage AI risks to people, organizations and society and to build trustworthiness in. The FAQ says (as reported by fetch) "NIST has produced the AI RMF as a voluntary Framework."
- AI RMF 1.0 released 2023-01-26; Generative AI Profile NIST-AI-600-1 released 2024-07-26.
- The page says the framework is being revised as part of the White House AI Action Plan.
- The core functions Govern, Map, Measure, Manage appeared only in an image caption: not confirmed in text, not stated as sourced.

EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (European Commission page):
- Four risk levels: unacceptable (banned practices, e.g. social scoring); high-risk (obligations including risk management, data quality, logging, documentation, human oversight); transparency risk (e.g. disclose chatbots, label deepfakes); minimal risk.
- In force 1 August 2024. Phased application dates listed: 2 Feb 2025 prohibitions and AI literacy; 2 Aug 2025 governance and general-purpose AI obligations; 2 Aug 2026 general applicability; 2 Dec 2027 high-risk Annex III; 2 Aug 2028 high-risk AI in regulated products (Annex I).
- AI Omnibus: the Commission adopted the legislative proposal on 2025-11-19, political agreement 7 May 2026, in force 27 July 2026; per the page it delayed high-risk dates and added a ban on nudification apps (a ninth prohibition applying from December 2026). The page's Annex I and Annex III dates reflect the Omnibus; the page gives no original dates.
