# EU Commission - AI Act Regulatory Framework

## Why it is reliable (or not)

Official EU institution page summarizing Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. It is a summary, not the legal text (the Official Journal text was not opened). Highly fast-changing: dates were shifted by the AI Omnibus; always recheck.

## Extracted points

- Four risk levels: unacceptable (banned practices, e.g. social scoring), high-risk (strict obligations: risk management, data quality, logging, documentation, human oversight), transparency risk (e.g. disclose chatbots, label deepfakes), minimal risk.
- Entered into force 1 August 2024. Application: 2 Feb 2025 prohibitions and AI literacy; 2 Aug 2025 governance and general-purpose AI obligations; 2 Aug 2026 general applicability; 2 Dec 2027 high-risk Annex III; 2 Aug 2028 high-risk AI in regulated products (Annex I).
- AI Omnibus: proposed 19 Nov 2025, political agreement 7 May 2026, in force 27 July 2026; delayed high-risk dates and added a ban on nudification apps (as stated on the page; ninth prohibition applies December 2026).
