# NIST AI Risk Management Framework

Pages read: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework and the FAQ https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework/ai-risk-management-framework-faqs.

## Why it is reliable (or not)

Official US standards body. Fast-changing: the page says the framework is being revised as part of the White House AI Action Plan, so verify the current version.

## Extracted points

- Voluntary framework to manage risks of AI to people, organizations and society, and to build trustworthiness into AI products and systems.
- AI RMF 1.0 (2023-01-26); Generative AI Profile NIST-AI-600-1 (2024-07-26).
- Core functions Govern, Map, Measure, Manage: the fetched page named them only in an image caption; to confirm in the AI RMF 1.0 document.
- FAQ: framework is voluntary, not required.
