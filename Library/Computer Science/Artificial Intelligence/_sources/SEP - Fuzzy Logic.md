# SEP - Fuzzy Logic

## Why it is reliable (or not)

Refereed Stanford encyclopedia entry by logicians. It is mathematical/logical (mathematical fuzzy logic) rather than an engineering introduction; use for definitions of degrees of truth and t-norms. Only a summary of the page was read via fetch.

## Extracted points

- Fuzzy sets assign degrees of membership in [0,1].
- Conjunction is modeled by t-norms (minimum, product, Lukasiewicz are standard examples).
- Per search snippet (not directly confirmed on page read): disjunction by t-conorms, negation 1-x.

## Relevant excerpts

> "A fuzzy set assigns a degree of membership, typically a real number from the interval [0,1], to elements of a universe." (as reported by fetch)
