# Newman-Wolfe - Packet Switching (CEN 4500C)

## Why it is reliable (or not)

University course notes. Level B. Dated (1995) and qualitative: no formulas for delays. Use together with Peterson and Davie (latency components) and the formulas below as a lead to confirm.

## Extracted points

- (3) Circuit switching reserves path before data; packet switching splits data into packets routed separately, shares links, pipelines; VC vs datagram services. Transmission, propagation, queuing delays are discussed qualitatively; propagation often neglected vs transmission "not always a safe assumption".
- Formulas d_trans = L/R and d_prop = d/s appear in other course PDFs listed by search (UT Austin CS326E, Buffalo CSE 589) but those were NOT opened (PDF), so not recorded as sources.

## Relevant excerpts
