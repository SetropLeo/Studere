# Newman-Wolfe - Transmission (CEN 4500C)

## Why it is reliable (or not)

University course notes, classical content (Nyquist, Shannon do not change). Level B. Dated 1995 and terse; media list is outdated (e.g. no modern Ethernet cabling). Good only for the capacity formulas.

## Extracted points

- (2) Notation "lg = log base 2" is stated by the source. Nyquist noiseless: R = 2H lg L (H bandwidth Hz, L signal levels). Shannon with white noise: R < H lg(1+S/N); SNR in dB = 10 log(S/N); practical systems reach about 1/4 to 1/2 of the limit. Media: twisted pair, coax, fibre (multi/single-mode), wireless types. Modulation (ASK, PSK, FSK) bandwidth formulas. Line coding not covered here (see Systems Approach note).

## Relevant excerpts

> "usually only 1/4 to 1/2 the Shannon limit can be attained in practice." (Shannon section)
