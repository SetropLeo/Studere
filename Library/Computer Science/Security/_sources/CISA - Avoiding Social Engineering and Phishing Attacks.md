# CISA - Avoiding Social Engineering and Phishing Attacks

## Why it is reliable (or not)

US government cybersecurity agency; defensive guidance. Short public-awareness article, release date as stated on the page (2021).

## Extracted points

- Social engineering: manipulating people into revealing information about an organization or its systems; attackers pose as employees, repair staff, researchers.
- Phishing: social engineering via email or fake websites impersonating a trusted organization; related: vishing (voice), smishing (SMS).
- Defenses: verify through a separate known channel, do not share sensitive data on request, check warning signs (mismatched sender, generic greeting, mismatched links, unexpected attachments), keep filters/antivirus current, enable multifactor authentication, report incidents.

## Relevant excerpts

> "Phishing is a form of social engineering."
