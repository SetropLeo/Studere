## Extracted points

- No single agreed definition: privacy as right to be left alone (Warren and Brandeis 1890), control over information (Westin 1967, Fried 1968), inaccessibility (Gavison 1980), cluster concept (DeCew 1997), contextual integrity (Nissenbaum 2010), "concept in disarray" (Solove 2008).
- Informational privacy: norms letting people control who knows what about them; German "informational self-determination" (1983).

## Relevant excerpts

> "An individual enjoys perfect privacy when he is completely inaccessible to others." (Gavison, quoted in the entry)
