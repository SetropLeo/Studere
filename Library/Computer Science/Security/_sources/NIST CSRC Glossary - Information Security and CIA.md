# NIST CSRC Glossary - Information Security

## Why it is reliable (or not)

Official US standards body; the page quotes definitions with their original publication (FIPS 200, SP 800-66 Rev. 2) citing the US legal code. Authoritative for definitions; US-centric.

## Extracted points

- Information security: protecting information and systems from unauthorized access, use, disclosure (continued beyond the page excerpt: disruption, modification, destruction).
- Confidentiality: preserving authorized restrictions on access and disclosure.
- Integrity: guarding against improper modification or destruction.
- Availability: timely and reliable access to and use of information.

## Relevant excerpts

> "integrity, which means guarding against improper information modification or destruction" (SP 800-66r2, citing 44 U.S.C. 3542)

> "availability, which means ensuring timely and reliable access to and use of information" (same)
