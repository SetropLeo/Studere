# GDPR Article 5 (gdpr-info.eu)

## Why it is reliable (or not)

Unofficial site reproducing the regulation text, so level B; the authoritative text is on EUR-Lex (the EUR-Lex summary page returned no content, so it was not verified). Regulation is fast-changing context (e.g. the digital omnibus proposals), so a review date is set. The application date above is general knowledge, not from this page: verify.

## Extracted points

- Six principles: lawfulness, fairness and transparency; purpose limitation; data minimisation; accuracy; storage limitation; integrity and confidentiality.
- Article 5(2): the controller is responsible for and must demonstrate compliance (accountability).
- Rights, lawful bases, breach notification and penalties are NOT covered (gap).

## Relevant excerpts

> "data minimisation" and "storage limitation" (principle headings, Art. 5(1))
