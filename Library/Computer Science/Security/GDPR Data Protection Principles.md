# GDPR Data Protection Principles

> **Summary:** Article 5 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 sets six principles for processing personal data plus an accountability duty. Source is an unofficial reproduction (gdpr-info.eu); EUR-Lex was not verified. Not legal advice.

## What it is

Article 5(1) principles:
1. lawfulness, fairness and transparency;
2. purpose limitation;
3. data minimisation;
4. accuracy;
5. storage limitation;
6. integrity and confidentiality.

Headings "data minimisation" and "storage limitation" are verbatim from the source note. Article 5(2): the controller is responsible for, and must be able to demonstrate, compliance (accountability).

> **Controller**: the party responsible under Art. 5(2); its definition is not in the read text (gloss, unsourced).

## How it works

The sixth principle overlaps with the security properties in [[Security Properties - Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability]] (own inference from the names). Rights, lawful bases, breach notification and penalties are NOT covered.
