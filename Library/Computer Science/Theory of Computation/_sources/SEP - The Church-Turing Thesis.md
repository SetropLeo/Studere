## Why it is reliable (or not)

Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, expert-authored and revised (latest 2023). Level A. Caveat: strong historical/philosophical focus (including the distinction between the thesis and stronger physical claims); not a technical tutorial.

## Extracted points

- Subtopic 5: common formulation "every effective computation can be carried out by a Turing machine"; Church's version in terms of lambda-definability / recursive functions. A thesis, not a theorem.
- Turing machine: scanner and one-dimensional tape divided into squares, finite symbol set, finite set of internal states (Turing's "m-configurations").
- Universal machine: Turing's all-purpose machine can imitate any single-purpose Turing machine; the description of that machine is stored on its tape as a finite list of instructions.
